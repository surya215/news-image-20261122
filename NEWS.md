## Lead story

**Iran strikes U.S.-linked sites in Bahrain (overnight into Sunday)**

- Reuters (via NationPress, 22 Nov 2026): Iran's IRGC fired missiles and
  drones at U.S. Patriot air-defence radars at two air bases in Bahrain on
  22 November; Bahrain's Interior Ministry confirmed an Iranian attack hit
  a civilian industrial facility and started a fire; Bahrain's Defence Force
  said its air defences intercepted a number of the attacks, with no
  injuries reported.
- AFP (22 Nov 2026, via NationWire): the IRGC launched the strikes
  overnight; the IRGC claimed it targeted air-defence systems at two U.S.
  military installations in Bahrain; residents reported smoke over Sitra
  Island.
- Al Jazeera (21–22 Nov 2026): the attacks came late Saturday night;
  Bahrain's ministry warned residents, then said an Iranian attack had
  struck a civilian industrial facility; U.S. officials said nearly all
  incoming missiles and drones were intercepted, with no immediate reports
  of casualties or damage to American installations.
